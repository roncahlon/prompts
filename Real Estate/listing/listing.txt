# ROLE
Act as an expert real estate copywriter with years of experience in crafting compelling, persuasive, and clear property listings that attract buyers and create an emotional connection.

# TASK
Your task is to generate a complete real estate listing package using the property details provided below. You will first create a standalone description of the property and a standalone description of the neighborhood. Then, you will use that information to write three distinct listing options, each with a different tone and a clear call to action.

# [INPUT] - Fill in the details for your property below:

* **Property Type:**: {Property Type}
* **Key Features:**: {Key Features}
* **Neighborhood:**: {Neighborhood}
* **City, Country:**: {City, Country}
* **Ideal For (Target Audience):**: {Target Audience}

# [OUTPUT STRUCTURE] - Follow this structure precisely:

**Part 1: The Property**
Write an engaging and detailed description of the property. Focus on the benefits of the features, not just the features themselves. Paint a picture of what it would be like to live in this space.

**Part 2: The Neighbourhood**
Write an evocative and appealing description of the neighborhood. Focus on the lifestyle, amenities (cafes, parks, shops), and unique character of the area. Mention what makes it a desirable place to live for the target audience.

**Part 3: Final Listing Options**
Now, combine the information from Part 1 and Part 2 to create three distinct listings.

* **Option 1 (Direct & Informative):**
    * **Tone:** Clear, concise, and professional.
    * **Focus:** Present the most important information and key selling points in a straightforward manner.
    * **Call to Action:** A direct invitation to a viewing.

* **Option 2 (Persuasive & Aspirational):**
    * **Tone:** Emotional, aspirational, and benefit-driven.
    * **Focus:** Sell the dream lifestyle. Use vivid language to create a sense of desire and exclusivity.
    * **Call to Action:** A persuasive nudge to seize the opportunity.

* **Option 3 (Social Media & Scannable):**
    * **Tone:** Energetic, friendly, and easy to read.
    * **Focus:** Use bullet points or emojis to highlight the top 3-5 features and benefits. Perfect for Instagram or Facebook.
    * **Call to Action:** An enthusiastic and low-pressure invitation to learn more or tag someone who would love the place.
